About me

I am a researcher in applied mathematics with interests in numerical analysis, finite element methods, and scientific computing. My work focuses on surface PDEs, phase-field models, and computational methods for problems arising in fluid dynamics and biomolecular systems.

I completed my PhD working with Maxim Olshanskii and Annalisa Quaini (advisors). My dissertation is available here.

The department nominated my dissertation for the University Outstanding Dissertation Award.

Much of my research studies interface dynamics, vesicle models, and reduced-order approaches for complex PDE systems, with emphasis on stability, accuracy, and computational efficiency.
